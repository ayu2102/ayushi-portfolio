Hey, I'm Ayushi

I figure out why things don't work for people — and then fix them.

I'm curious by nature, a little obsessed with details, and genuinely care about the people using what I design. I check with developers before locking anything down, because a design that can't be built is just a pretty picture.

Right now I'm at The Bigword, leading the redesign of WordSynk — a language services platform used by enterprise clients, 20,000+ interpreters, and the UK Ministry of Justice. Three very different users, one platform. It's the kind of problem I find hard to put down.

I've worked across fintech, edtech, e-commerce, language tech, and more. The industry changes. The process doesn't — understand the problem first, design second.

Looking for a product design role where the problems are real and the team is honest.

About

I'm a product designer with 3+ years of working across SaaS, language tech, fintech, and edtech. I care about the people on the other side of the screen — and I tend to notice the details that most people scroll past.

I understand how products are built, not just how they should look. I check feasibility with developers, ask the uncomfortable questions in stakeholder meetings, and try to make sure what gets designed can actually ship.

I'm at a point where I want to go deeper — sharper research practice, more complex product problems, and a team that pushes me. If that sounds like your team, let's talk.
