FactSeal

Background Verification Platform

Role

Year
2022
Type
Mobile App + Internal Web Portal
Status
Completed

FactSeal began as a lightweight app focused mainly on location verification. The redesign expanded it into a complete background screening experience for companies, candidates, and individuals who needed a structured way to verify identity, education, employment, references, documents, consent, and location.

01

Context

FactSeal supports background verification during hiring. Companies can initiate checks for candidates, candidates can submit required information and documents, and individuals can also use the app for personal verification needs.

The product existed before the redesign, but its functionality was limited. It primarily handled current location verification. The redesign expanded both the product scope and the user experience.

02

Problem

The existing app solved only one part of the verification journey.

Background screening involves multiple sensitive steps: identity, education, employment history, certificates, references, consent, document upload, and location verification. Without a clear end-to-end flow, users could easily feel lost, unsure, or overwhelmed.

03

My Role

I redesigned the complete product experience.

05

Design Decisions

Guided Verification Flow

Instead of exposing users to the entire verification system at once, the experience guides them step by step through the required checks.

Clear Verification States

Each stage communicates what has been completed, what requires attention, and what the user needs to do next.

Sensitive Data, Clear Interaction

Because verification involves personal and professional information, the interface needed to feel structured, transparent, and reliable.

06

Redesigned Experience

The redesigned product included onboarding, OTP verification, consent authorization, identity checks, academic and employment verification flows, location confirmation, document upload, profile screens, and completion states.

  • The internal web portal was redesigned to help teams manage and review verification activity connected to the candidate-facing app.
  • Product screenshots were designed for App Store and Play Store listings across mobile and tablet formats.

07

Looking Back

This project taught me that enterprise product design is not just about adding more functionality. It is about organizing complex workflows into experiences that feel predictable, transparent, and easy to complete.
