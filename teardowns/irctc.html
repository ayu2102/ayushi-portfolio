Product teardown

IRCTC

Notes on urgency, trust, and high-demand booking flows where every unclear state feels expensive.

Observation

The product serves a high-pressure need, but users must decode availability, waitlist logic, timing, and payment risk while moving quickly.

Insight

When the stakes are practical and immediate, design has to make system truth visible. Ambiguity damages trust faster than visual roughness.

Opportunity

Use clearer sequencing, stronger status language, and visible recovery routes so people know where they are and what can still be done.
