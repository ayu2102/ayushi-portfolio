Product teardown

GPay

Notes on restraint, financial trust, and what happens when secondary surfaces gather around a core action.

Observation

The payment flow is simple, but the surrounding experience includes offers, rewards, finance prompts, and commerce features competing for attention.

Insight

In money movement, focus is a trust signal. People should never wonder whether the product's priorities are aligned with their task.

Opportunity

Keep the primary payment path visually and structurally protected, then introduce secondary actions only when they are contextual and easy to dismiss.
