01

The Product

WordSynk powers language services for governments, enterprise organizations, and thousands of professional linguists worldwide.

The platform supports the complete lifecycle of translation, transcription, and interpreting, from customer bookings to assignment management and operational coordination.

Its users range from public-sector organizations placing urgent interpreter requests to linguists accepting work on the move, making reliability and clarity essential to every workflow.

02

The Challenge

The platform had evolved over many years without a unified design direction.

While operationally successful, increasing feature complexity created friction across navigation, accessibility, and consistency, especially for users working under time pressure.

Accessibility

Critical workflows required clearer navigation and more accessible interaction patterns.

Visual Consistency

Patterns had evolved independently across products, creating inconsistent experiences across portals.

Multiple User Groups

Customers, linguists, and internal operations teams approached the platform with different goals, mental models, and levels of urgency.

03

Understanding the Ecosystem

The biggest design challenge was not aesthetics. It was designing a platform that could serve three distinct user groups without creating confusion between them.

Enterprise Clients

Organizations booking interpreting, translation, or transcription services. They need clarity, confidence, and speed, often while making requests under time pressure.

Professional Linguists

Interpreters and translators accepting, managing, and completing assignments. They need a clear, fast, mobile-friendly experience to review and act on work.

Internal Operations Team

Bigword's internal staff managing bookings, assignments, and delivery behind the scenes. They need dense operational information without unnecessary friction.

04

Discovery

Understanding before designing

Before jumping into solutions, discovery focused on understanding where the platform was creating friction and why.

Heuristic Evaluation Feedback Review Stakeholder Conversations Backlog Review

Heuristic Evaluation

Reviewed existing screens against usability principles to identify structural issues such as unclear navigation, weak hierarchy, and missing feedback states.

Feedback Review

Analysed existing user feedback and complaints to identify recurring patterns in what frustrated users most.

Stakeholder Conversations

Worked with Product Owners to understand business constraints, what must not break, and what success should look like for the company.

Backlog Review

Reviewed existing product requests and user stories to understand what pain points had already been captured by other teams.

05

Design Leadership

My role extended beyond interface redesign. I worked across discovery, experience design, stakeholder alignment, and design system consistency.

Discovery

Audited the existing experience and identified usability opportunities across key workflows.

Experience Design

Owned information architecture, user flows, wireframes, and high-fidelity interfaces for redesigned experiences.

Stakeholder Alignment

Presented design rationale, facilitated reviews, and iterated with Product Owners based on business and technical constraints.

Design System

Extended existing components while maintaining consistency across the broader WordSynk platform.

06

What Guided Every Decision

Every design decision had to respect the reality of enterprise systems: people depend on these workflows daily, and even small changes can affect operational continuity.

Clarity over cleverness Accessibility first Serve each user type distinctly Do not break what already works Support business continuity Reduce operational friction

Current Status

The redesign is currently in progress in collaboration with Product Owners and engineering teams.

The customer-facing experience is the current focus, with additional platform areas following as part of the broader modernization effort.

09

Reflection

Designing for enterprise is not about making interfaces simpler for the sake of simplicity.

It is about making complexity manageable for people whose work depends on making the right decision quickly.

Working on WordSynk reinforced that successful enterprise products balance usability, operational constraints, accessibility, and business continuity, not by removing complexity, but by organizing it.
